# Main Sequence 🌟

An incremental game where you *are* the star.

Gather a cloud. Light a fire that lasts ten billion years. End as everything.

- Click to accrete hydrogen; automate the infall with Bok globules, molecular clouds, accretion disks.
- Ignite the real fusion chain: proton–proton → triple-alpha → alpha ladder → oxygen burning → silicon burning.
- Iron doesn't burn. It piles up in your core until the Chandrasekhar limit — then **go supernova**.
- Stardust forged in the blast seeds your next star. Population II, CNO cycle, degenerate matter.
- Choose your death: collapse early for a **pulsar** (it automates your next life) or overshoot the TOV limit for a **black hole** (raw gravity, deeper offline time).
- The sky remembers. Every nebula you've ever been stays painted behind the next star you become.

Single self-contained HTML file, no dependencies. Open `main-sequence.html` in a browser and play.
